# Competition

**Market snapshot:** October 2026

## Fantasy Government

https://www.fantasygovernment.com/

Direct competitor.

Public product features include fantasy drafting, legislative scoring, leagues, weekly competition, player profiles, and a paid tier.

Its presentation leans heavily toward civics/education.

## Politiparty

https://politiparty.com/

Direct competitor.

Notable strengths:

- simple roster format
- weekly scoring
- highly explicit scoring rules
- source-backed point events
- strong "box scores for Congress" framing

## Fantasy Congress

https://fantasycongress.com/

Long-running example showing that fantasy Congress as a concept predates the current wave.

## Fantasy Elect

https://fantasyelect.io/

Adjacent rather than direct.

Focuses more on political prediction contests than drafting legislators and scoring congressional activity.

## What the market validates

This core loop is no longer unique:

> Draft Congress -> real activity -> fantasy score.

Multiple teams independently built toward the same concept.

## What Capitol League must do differently

Do not compete primarily as an education product.

Prioritize:

- Random Draft
- under-one-minute onboarding
- 30-second team checks
- immediate missed-vote/no-show pain
- sports-like player cards and matchups
- roster regret / sleepers / streaks
- simple explanations
- source-backed drill-down

The desired product is:

> **A game that accidentally makes people know what Congress is doing.**

## Traction

Public evidence confirms active competitors, but no publicly obvious category winner is established.

That leaves room, but the basic idea alone is not enough differentiation.
